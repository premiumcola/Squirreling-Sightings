{# Erkennungsprofil — one section, two views (Netze / Verlauf) rendered into #netzBody by static/js/netz/. Only one is in the DOM at a time, so the back button navigates between them for free and there is no modal-inside-a-modal on iOS. The Netz/Verlauf TAB BAR is gone: with only two views a full-width segmented control was more chrome than choice. #netzViewBtn top-right toggles both directions — it must stay a toggle, not a one-way link, because the archive list has no back control of its own. Internal ids/classes stay `netz*` throughout static/js/netz/ and 32-netz.css — only the user-visible text says "Erkennungsprofil". Renaming every internal identifier too would be a large, purely-cosmetic diff across a dozen files for zero behavioural gain. The static markup below is what a browser with JS disabled sees, and what is on screen for the moment between paint and hydration — deliberately the calm empty state, not a spinner. #}
